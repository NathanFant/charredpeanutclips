CharredPeanutClips Uploader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

CharredPeanutClips Uploader ("the app") is a private tool that uploads videos to the CharredPeanutClips YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel's owner. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app accesses

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to, and delete videos from, the channel owner's own YouTube channel. To do this it stores an OAuth access token and refresh token issued by Google for that one account.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process personal data about viewers or any other person.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.
  • It does not sell or share any data with third parties.

Storage and security

The OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and server, and are used solely to make the API calls described above. No YouTube data is stored beyond the IDs of the videos the app itself uploaded.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revoking access makes the stored tokens unusable; they are then deleted.

Third-party terms

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
